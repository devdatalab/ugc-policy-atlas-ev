Expected value model

What this page asks

The expected-value model supplies a fixed total worker benefit from direct engagement. This page holds that total fixed and asks how its welfare value changes when more of the benefit reaches people with lower consumption.

A gain to a lower-consumption household receives greater welfare weight. The result expresses consumption-equivalent welfare value; estimating household spending calls for a separate income-and-spending model.
