Distributional value

Optional, illustrative weighting of direct-engagement worker benefits by household consumption level.

Expected value model

What this page asks

The expected-value model estimates a fixed total worker benefit from direct engagement. This page does not change that total. It asks how its welfare value changes if more of the benefit reaches people with lower rather than higher consumption.

A gain to a lower-consumption household receives greater welfare weight. This is a consumption-equivalent welfare calculation—not a forecast of extra household spending or cash received.

How much more value for lower-consumption gains?

Evidence and limits

Baseline consumption uses all-India urban Monthly Per Capita Consumption Expenditure (MPCE) from India’s 2022–23 Household Consumption Expenditure Survey: bottom 40% ₹3,473, middle 40% ₹6,265, top 20% ₹13,132 per person per month. MoSPI HCES 2022–23, Statement 14

The underlying direct worker-benefit value remains the main model’s illustrative ADB-based benchmark. This page does not establish who receives gains, nor who bears policy costs; it therefore keeps policy costs outside the headline distributional result.
