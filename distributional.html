Weighting assumptions

Weighted value = Σ (benefit allocated to group × (urban median consumption / group consumption)η).

How to interpret this

This is a consumption-equivalent welfare weighting, not a forecast of extra household spending. It gives more value to an equal monetary gain reaching people with lower baseline consumption.

Baseline consumption uses all-India urban Monthly Per Capita Consumption Expenditure (MPCE) from India’s 2022–23 Household Consumption Expenditure Survey: bottom 40% ₹3,473, middle 40% ₹6,265, top 20% ₹13,132 per person per month. MoSPI HCES 2022–23, Statement 14

The underlying direct worker-benefit value remains the main model’s illustrative ADB-based benchmark. This page does not establish who receives gains, nor does it weight open-platform benefits.
